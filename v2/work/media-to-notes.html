1 问题Problem

听完就散。

一集两小时的课，真正有用的可能就三处。想回去查某一点，只能重听、拖进度条、再猜位置。

已经有转写工具了，但转写不等于笔记：两万字的逐字稿，读起来和自己重听一遍差不了多少。

Listen once, lose it.

Two hours of lecture, maybe three useful passages. To check one of them you replay, scrub, guess the timestamp.

Transcription tools already exist, but a transcript isn't a note. Twenty thousand characters read about as well as listening again.

2 我的判断My call

先问要哪一种笔记，再按这个类型写。

详细整理、重点摘要、逐字稿、专题整理、会议纪要——这五种东西的信息结构完全不同。用一套模板生成，结果一定四不像。

第二个判断：不确定的内容不补写。人名、数字、截止日期拿不准就标注，不猜。检查过结构不等于逐句核对过——这句要写进交付说明。

Ask which kind of note first, then write to that shape.

Detailed notes, executive summary, raw transcript, topic digest, meeting minutes — five different information structures. One template produces something that is none of them.

Second call: never fill an uncertainty. Unclear names, figures and deadlines get marked, not guessed. Checking the structure is not checking every sentence — that belongs in the delivery note.

3 取舍与代价Trade-offs

放弃了「一键出笔记」。

一键体验更好，但输出必然是通用形态。我选了先问一次，多一次交互，换结构对得上需求。

第二个取舍：默认本地转写。云端更快，但音视频要离开使用者的机器。所以默认本地，需要云端时先说明要上传什么、大概多少钱。

I gave up the one-click note.

One click feels better and always produces the generic shape. I take one extra question so the structure matches the request.

Second trade: local transcription by default. Cloud is faster, but the recording leaves the user's machine. So local first; when cloud is needed, state what gets uploaded and roughly what it costs.

4 怎么验证How I verified

四类检查：

  1. 关键事实、时间码、链接是否可核对
  2. 截图是否贴在对应文字旁，并说明画面内容
  3. 外部资料是否标了来源，原片内容和补充资料是否分开
  4. 未完成部分是否写明

判断标准不是「读起来顺」，是能不能靠它回到原片的具体位置。

Four checks:

  1. Are the key facts, timecodes and links verifiable?
  2. Are screenshots placed beside the text they illustrate, and described?
  3. Are outside sources attributed, and kept separate from what the recording said?
  4. Is the incomplete part stated?

The bar isn't “reads smoothly”. It's can you get back to the exact moment in the original.

6 如果重来If I did it again

我会把「追问」做成 Skill 的一部分。

现在它是单次交付：给素材，出笔记。但真实使用里，看完笔记一定会想「这段原话是什么」。如果 Skill 能记住时间码并支持追问，笔记就从静态文档变成可对话的索引。

这比继续细化五种笔记模板更有价值。

I'd make follow-up questions part of the skill.

Today it's a single delivery: material in, note out. In real use you always want “what exactly did they say there”. If the skill kept the timecodes and answered follow-ups, the note would become a queryable index instead of a static document.

That's worth more than refining the five templates further.
