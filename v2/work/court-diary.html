1 问题Problem

打球时手机架在球场边，人不会去看屏幕。

但赛后一段完整录像让「挑出好球」变成苦差：从头拉到尾，反复对时间点。资料里确认的两个卡点很具体——录完不知道干嘛，和挑球累。

所以问题不是「怎么录下来」，是「录完之后怎么让人愿意再看一遍」。

During a match the phone sits by the court and nobody looks at it.

Afterwards a full recording makes “find the good points” a chore: scrub, guess, re-scrub. Two frictions came out of the research — recording finishes and you don't know what next, and picking is tiring.

So the question isn't “how do we record”. It's “how do we make someone want to watch it again”.

2 我的判断My call

主任务只有一个：打球。任何在场上要求看屏幕的设计都是错的。

我把流程按「是否打断打球」分两半：场上只允许一个动作——标记；所有挑选、剪辑、分享全推到赛后。

第二个判断是录制状态必须可信。开始后短暂保留画面，让人一眼确认「录上了」；中断时说明已录下的内容和下一步。

There is one primary task: playing. Any design that asks you to look at a screen mid-match is wrong.

I split the flow on a single axis — does it interrupt play. On court, one action only: mark. Picking, trimming and sharing all move after the match.

The second call: recording state must be trustworthy. Keep the frame briefly after start so you can confirm it's running; on an interruption, say what was already captured and what happens next.

3 取舍与代价Trade-offs

放弃了「场上功能丰富」的想象。

很容易想到在场上做集锦、加语音备注、实时统计。每加一个，都在跟打球抢注意力。

代价：场上能做的事变少，产品看起来「不智能」。换来的是主任务不被打断——这是这个产品的全部价值。

I gave up the fantasy of a feature-rich courtside app.

It's easy to imagine highlights, voice notes, live stats. Each one competes with playing for attention.

The cost: less you can do on court, and the product looks less clever. What it buys: the primary task stays uninterrupted — which is the whole point.

4 怎么验证How I verified

三个可观察的信号：

  1. 开始录制后，用户能否立刻确认「在录」
  2. 赛后从打开到看到第一个标记片段，需要几次操作
  3. 中断发生后，用户是否知道已有内容和下一步

第 2 条是关键：如果挑选仍需翻长视频，整个设计就没成立。

Three observable signals:

  1. After start, can the user immediately confirm it's recording?
  2. From opening to first marked point, how many interactions?
  3. After an interruption, does the user know what was captured and what's next?

The second is the load-bearing one. If picking still means scrubbing a long video, the design failed.

5 结果与边界What works, what doesn't

已完成：完整旅程的交互设计、录制状态的可信表达、收工时刻的下一步、单球页信息结构（速度、旋转、轨迹放在一起决定留不留）。

还不存在：可安装的 iOS / Apple Watch 版本，可在线试用的 Demo。速度、旋转、轨迹是概念描述，不是已实现能力。

Done: the full journey, a trustworthy recording state, the “finish” moment with a clear next step, and the single-point page structure (speed, spin, trajectory together, so you can decide to keep or drop).

Not built: no installable iOS / Apple Watch build, no online demo. Speed, spin and trajectory are concept descriptions, not shipped capability.

This page is a concept and interaction spec, not a released product.

6 如果重来If I did it again

我会先做一个只有「录 + 标记」的可运行原型，在真实球场上用一次。

因为整个设计赌在一个假设上：人会在打球间隙点一下手机。这个假设没被真实验证。如果实际没人愿意点，后面所有赛后体验都不重要。

用真机验证这一个动作，比继续完善赛后的信息层级更有价值。

I'd build a prototype with only record and mark, and use it on a real court once.

The whole design bets on one assumption: that someone will tap a phone between points. That hasn't been tested. If nobody taps, none of the post-match experience matters.

Validating that single action on device is worth more than polishing the post-match hierarchy.
