1 问题Problem

财报研究里最费时间的不是找数字，是确认这个数字算不算数。

同一家公司，不同报告期、不同币种、不同口径，能算出完全不同的市值和利润率。模型可能答对一个数字，却配错期间；也可能把材料里没写的原因，讲得像已经查实。

结果是：答案看着顺，研究者仍要打开原文重查一遍。

The expensive part of earnings research isn't finding the number. It's confirming the number counts.

Same company, different period, different currency, different definition — three different market caps. A model can get the figure right and the period wrong, or explain a cause with a confidence the filing never had.

So the answer reads well, and the researcher re-opens the source anyway.

2 我的判断My call

我把三件事拆开：事实、计算、解释。

  • 事实来自披露文件，附时间和口径
  • 计算走确定性流程，保留过程
  • 解释交给模型，但必须标注它是解释

模型不负责「记住」财务数字，负责组织问题、检索材料、把解释写清楚。

I separate three things: facts, computation, interpretation.

  • Facts come from filings, with a date and a definition attached
  • Computation runs deterministically, keeping its working
  • Interpretation goes to the model, but is labelled as interpretation

The model doesn't hold the numbers. It frames the question, retrieves the material, and writes the explanation.

3 取舍与代价Trade-offs

放弃了「一句话给结论」的产品形态。

只给结论的金融 AI 用起来更爽，也更容易让人误以为结论可靠。我选了更长的回答：每个数字指回来源，缺失材料明确说缺失。

代价是答案变长、下结论更保守、首次响应变慢（实测 20–45 秒）。我接受，因为金融场景里「看起来确定但错了」的损失，远大于「多花半分钟看清来源」。

I gave up the one-line answer.

A financial AI that just says “buy” is more satisfying, and easier to trust by mistake. I chose the longer shape: every figure points back to a source, every missing input is named.

The cost: longer answers, fewer confident conclusions, a slower first response (measured 20–45s). I accept it — in finance, “confidently wrong” costs far more than half a minute spent checking the source.

4 怎么验证How I verified

把「答错了」拆成四类，分别检查：

类型检查什么
检索错误找到的文件是不是这家公司、这个期间
计算错误同比、利润率的口径和过程对不对
无依据解释解释有没有对应的材料支持
信息缺口缺的材料有没有被明确说出来

第四类最容易被漏掉：模型不会「不知道」，它会补一个听起来合理的原因。

“Wrong” is four different failures, checked separately:

TypeWhat's checked
RetrievalIs this the right company, the right period?
ComputationDo the margin and YoY figures keep their definitions?
Unsupported explanationDoes the cause trace to material?
Missing inputWas the absent data actually named?

The fourth is the one these products skip: a model is never “uninformed”, it supplies a plausible cause.

5 结果与边界What works, what doesn't

已经做到的：公开测试版可在网页直接提问；回答带来源、数据时间和缺口说明；快速研究模式可用。

还不行：深入研究模式在公开测试期间关闭；数据源覆盖不保证每只股票都有全部字段；行情为延迟或历史数据，不是交易所实时报价；访客共享同一空间，没有账号隔离。

演示入口不代表所有计划中的数据源、记忆和评测能力都已完成。

Working now: a public beta you can query in the browser; answers carry sources, data timestamps and stated gaps; quick research mode is available.

Not yet: deep research is off during the public beta; source coverage doesn't guarantee every field for every ticker; quotes are delayed or historical, not exchange-live; visitors share one space with no account isolation.

The demo entry does not mean every planned data source, memory and evaluation capability is finished.

6 如果重来If I did it again

我会更早把「评测」做成产品的一部分，而不是事后补的检查表。

现在评测是写在外面的方法论；如果它变成界面上可见的信号——这个答案的哪一段没有被来源支持——用户就能自己判断该信多少。这比在文档里承诺「我们很严谨」有用得多。

数据来自 Yahoo Finance / yfinance、Finnhub、AkShare、SEC EDGAR、同花顺 iFinD。均为公开测试版说明，不是性能测试结论。FinSearch-X 是本项目前身，已合并，不再单独列出。

I'd make evaluation part of the product earlier, instead of a checklist applied afterwards.

Right now evaluation is a method described outside the product. If it surfaced as a visible signal — which sentence in this answer has no source behind it — the reader could judge for themselves. That's worth more than promising rigour in a document.
