02 / 方法how i work
三条判断准则。Three working principles.
01

漏掉该识别的，比多调一次模型更贵。 Missing text you should have read costs more than one extra call.

在理赔影像分流里，我用「需 OCR 召回率」验收，而不是分类准确率。 In claims routing I validated against recall on images that need OCR, not classification accuracy.

代价：多花时间标注漏检样本。 Cost: more time labelling the misses.

→ 见理赔影像智能分流→ Claims Image Routing
02

事实、计算、解释必须分开。 Facts, computation and interpretation stay separate.

financial-bot 的回答里，每个数字都指回披露文件和时间；材料没写的，就说没写。 Every number in financial-bot points back to a filing and a date. If the material doesn't say it, the answer doesn't claim it.

代价：答案更长，也更少下结论。 Cost: longer answers, fewer confident conclusions.

→ 见 financial-bot→ financial-bot
03

记录不能打断被记录的事。 Recording must not interrupt what is being recorded.

Court Diary 把复杂操作全挪到赛后，场上只留一个标记动作。 Court Diary moves every complex operation to after the match, leaving one marking action on court.

代价：场上能做的事变少。 Cost: less you can do on court.

→ 见 Court Diary→ Court Diary
